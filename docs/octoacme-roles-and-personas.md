# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Architecture Owner

### Role Summary
Technical Architecture Owners ensure technical feasibility and system design alignment. They provide technical leadership and oversight to guide architectural decisions and maintain design consistency across projects.

### Responsibilities
- Evaluate technical feasibility of proposed features and initiatives
- Design and review system architecture and technical solutions
- Ensure alignment with technical standards and best practices
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and dependencies
- Mentor Developers on architectural patterns and design principles
- Participate in design reviews and technical decision-making

### Goals
- Maintain a scalable, maintainable, and performant technical architecture
- Reduce technical debt and system complexity
- Enable rapid feature delivery through well-designed systems
- Foster knowledge sharing and architectural consistency

### Key Interactions
- **Project Manager**: Provides feasibility assessments and technical risk analysis during planning
- **Developers**: Reviews architectural designs, mentors on technical implementation, participates in code reviews
- **Product Manager**: Discusses technical trade-offs and constraints affecting product roadmap
- **QA Owner**: Collaborates on testability and quality considerations in architectural decisions

### Decision Authority
- Technical approval for architectural decisions and design patterns
- Authority to escalate technical risks and request mitigation strategies
- Approval of technology selections and framework choices

### Typical Communication
- Architectural design documents and decision records
- Technical review meetings and design discussions
- Risk and dependency assessments
- Mentoring sessions and knowledge-sharing forums

---

## Quality Assurance Owner

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Owners define and execute QA strategy, establish quality standards, and ensure products meet quality expectations. They lead testing efforts and drive continuous quality improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Define QA strategy, test plans, and quality metrics for projects
- Establish testing standards and acceptance criteria
- Oversee test coverage and identify quality gaps
- Collaborate with Developers on testability and quality concerns
- Coordinate with Project Manager on quality milestones and release readiness
- Drive process improvements to enhance quality and efficiency
- Report on quality metrics and product readiness

### Goals
- Ensure consistent product quality and reliability
- Achieve high test coverage and prevent defects
- Reduce quality-related risks and rework
- Maintain customer trust and satisfaction

### Key Interactions
- **Developers**: Collaborates on test requirements, code review from quality perspective, assists with test automation
- **Project Manager**: Communicates quality status, escalates quality risks, confirms release readiness
- **Technical Architecture Owner**: Advises on testability considerations in architectural decisions
- **Product Manager**: Validates quality against acceptance criteria and customer expectations

### Decision Authority
- Quality gate approvals and release readiness decisions
- Authority to halt releases if quality standards are not met
- Approval of test plans and quality acceptance criteria
- Control over quality metrics and improvement initiatives

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and QA strategy documents
- Quality status reports and metrics dashboards
- Quality gate reviews and release sign-offs
- Test results and defect reports

---

## Risk & Compliance Manager

### Role Summary
Risk & Compliance Managers identify, analyze, track, and mitigate project risks. They ensure compliance with organizational policies, regulatory requirements, and industry standards.

### Responsibilities
- Identify and assess project risks throughout the project lifecycle
- Develop and execute risk mitigation strategies
- Maintain risk registers and track risk status
- Report risk status to Project Manager and steering committee
- Ensure compliance with organizational policies and regulatory requirements
- Escalate high-impact risks requiring executive attention
- Facilitate risk discussions and retrospective learning

### Goals
- Minimize project disruptions from unforeseen risks
- Ensure regulatory and compliance adherence
- Improve organizational risk awareness and management practices
- Reduce costly rework and schedule delays from unmanaged risks

### Key Interactions
- **Project Manager**: Reports to PM on risk status, escalates critical risks, collaborates on mitigation planning
- **Developers**: Collaborates on technical risk identification and mitigation strategies
- **Stakeholders/Executives**: Advises steering committee and leadership on project-level risks
- **Technical Architecture Owner**: Discusses technical and architectural risks

### Decision Authority
- Risk escalation and mitigation strategy approval
- Authority to recommend project adjustments based on risk assessment
- Compliance verification and policy exception approvals (in consultation with leadership)
- Risk register maintenance and status reporting authority

### Typical Communication
- Risk registers and risk assessment reports
- Risk escalation alerts and mitigation plans
- Compliance checklists and policy documentation
- Executive risk briefings and steering committee reports

---

## Stakeholder Liaison

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Liaisons manage external stakeholder expectations, facilitate communication, and ensure stakeholder feedback is incorporated into project priorities. They bridge internal project teams and external stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Identify and maintain stakeholder register and engagement plan
- Facilitate regular stakeholder communication and feedback sessions
- Translate stakeholder feedback into actionable insights for Product Manager
- Manage stakeholder expectations and concerns
- Communicate project status and impacts to stakeholders
- Coordinate stakeholder reviews and validation activities
- Escalate critical stakeholder issues and risks to Project Manager

### Goals
- Maximize stakeholder satisfaction and engagement
- Ensure stakeholder voice is heard in prioritization decisions
- Reduce stakeholder-driven disruptions and scope changes
- Build strong partnerships and long-term relationships

### Key Interactions
- **Project Manager**: Escalates stakeholder concerns and priorities, coordinates stakeholder communications
- **Product Manager**: Influences product roadmap priorities based on stakeholder feedback and business value
- **Developers**: Facilitates stakeholder validation and user feedback on deliverables
- **All Team Members**: Represents external perspective in decision-making discussions

### Decision Authority
- Stakeholder concern prioritization and escalation decisions
- Authority to request project adjustments based on critical stakeholder input
- Stakeholder communication and engagement approval
- Feedback incorporation recommendations to Product Manager

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder meeting notes and feedback summaries
- Status reports tailored to stakeholder interests
- Escalation alerts for critical stakeholder concerns
- Feedback incorporation proposals

---

## Knowledge Manager

### Role Summary
Knowledge Managers capture, document, and disseminate project knowledge and lessons learned. They ensure organizational learning and support team development through knowledge sharing and documentation.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain project documentation standards
- Capture key decisions, design rationales, and lessons learned
- Facilitate retrospectives and knowledge-sharing sessions
- Maintain project knowledge repository and accessibility
- Support onboarding and team member ramp-up with documentation
- Identify and address knowledge gaps
- Drive continuous improvement through documented insights

### Goals
- Preserve and share organizational knowledge
- Reduce knowledge silos and improve collaboration
- Accelerate onboarding and team productivity
- Enable evidence-based process improvements

### Key Interactions
- **All Team Members**: Collects insights, facilitates retrospectives, supports documentation efforts
- **Project Manager**: Partners on retrospective facilitation and process improvement identification
- **Developers**: Documents technical decisions, architecture rationales, and implementation insights
- **Product Manager**: Documents product decisions and market learnings

### Decision Authority
- Documentation standards and knowledge repository structure approval
- Authority to request documentation and knowledge capture activities
- Guidelines for lessons learned documentation and retrospective insights
- Knowledge accessibility and sharing method decisions

### Typical Communication
- Knowledge management framework and documentation standards
- Retrospective notes and lessons learned summaries
- Technical knowledge base and decision records
- Onboarding guides and team learning resources
